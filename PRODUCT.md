## Platform

## Stack

## Users

Primary: recruiters and HR skimming for fit and keywords, who then pass David to hiring managers. Secondary: hiring managers at FDM's client firms deciding on a Business Analyst or Project/Program Manager. Both give the page about 60 seconds, often on a phone.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for David Salazar that gets him interviews for Business Analyst and Project/Program Manager roles (Operations Analyst, Project Coordinator and Associate PM also in scope). Success: a reader understands in under a minute what he does, sees proof, and contacts him or downloads the resume.

Layout and tab-style navigation inspired by nahiyan.codes (layout only, not its look). Avoid grain overlays, gradient blobs, generic card grids, lime-on-black, and the old davesal.me look. ## Product Principles

1. Say what David does in the first screen, then prove it.
2. Be honest about limits; the case studies state what was and wasn't done.
3. Plain words over polish; a reader should never have to decode copy.
4. Fast and readable on a phone before anything else.
5. One source of truth for deploys.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Readable contrast on the beige palette, full keyboard access (including the tab navigation), visible focus, reduced-motion respected, usable at phone width with no horizontal scroll.
